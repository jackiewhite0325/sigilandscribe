Help shape Off Leash

This zone is for teens, so teens should help build it. These are ways to contribute. Nothing goes live without a review first.

We are trying this out. If it works, it stays. If it does not, we will change it. Your feedback on the process itself counts too.

How contributing works

  1. Pick a role below that sounds like you.
  2. Send your contribution by email. You can use a nickname if you prefer.
  3. It gets reviewed. Every submission is read by a real person before anything happens with it. Nothing is automatic.
  4. You will hear back about whether it is a fit, and why or why not.
Share your story

Story Contributor

Write about something you have lived through. It does not have to be dramatic. Small true things count.

  • A hard day and what got you through it
  • Something about your body or brain that you wish people understood
  • A moment you felt truly seen by someone

Stories are 300 to 800 words. You choose whether to use your first name, a nickname, or stay anonymous.

Coping Tool Tester

Try the tools in the coping library and say honestly what worked, what did not, and what felt off.

  • Pick any tool from the coping micro-library
  • Try it on a real hard day (not a good day)
  • Tell us: did it help even a little? Did anything feel wrong about it?

Your feedback shapes which tools stay, which get rewritten, and which get removed.

Keep it real

Wording Reviewer

Read pages on Off Leash and flag any wording that feels preachy, fake, condescending, or just off.

  • Quote the line that bugged you
  • Say why in your own words (one sentence is enough)
  • Suggest a better way to say it, if you want to

You know when an adult is trying too hard. That instinct is the whole job.

Topic Suggester

Tell us what you wish someone would write about. What is missing? What do you need that is not here?

  • A topic for a new story
  • A coping tool for a situation we have not covered
  • A question you wish had an honest answer somewhere

One line is enough. "Someone should write about ___" is a complete suggestion.

Safety and privacy

  • Nothing is automatic. Every submission is read by a person. Nothing goes on the site without review.
  • Use a nickname if you want. You never have to use your real name.
  • No personal details. Please do not include your last name, school, address, or phone number.
  • You can withdraw anytime. Changed your mind about something you sent? Say so and it will be removed.
  • This is not a crisis service. If you are in crisis, please reach out to someone who can help right now (see below).

If this moment feels bigger than a contribution form: call or text 988 (US). You matter, and support is there.
