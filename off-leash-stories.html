Why I wrote these

I wanted to make stories where the teen who is usually treated like the problem gets to be a whole person instead.

Not a lesson. Not an inspiration story. Just someone living through a real day, getting some things wrong, knowing some things everyone else missed, and finding people who do not ask them to become smaller.

You deserve stories that trust you with the truth.

Jackie
