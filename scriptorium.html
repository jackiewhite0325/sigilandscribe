The Scriptorium

One room for writers, in two weathers. Settle in with a breathing light and a blank line, or spin the machines and make a little mischief. The old Quiet Room and Chaos Room live here now, half and half.

Why the name? A scriptorium was the scribes' writing room. This one is yours.

Settle

Quiet creative time.

Nothing here asks you to finish a task, buy a book, or improve. Use a pen, a note app, the spaces below, or only your attention. Stay as long as you like.

Breathing light

If you arrived frazzled, start here. This light breathes slowly with you: it grows, rests, then eases back.

  1. Tap the light to begin.
  2. Breathe in as the light grows.
  3. Hold while the light rests.
  4. Breathe out as it eases back.

Repeat for a few rounds, or just watch for a moment. Look away whenever you like, and tap the light again to pause. If you feel dizzy or unwell, stop, look away, and breathe normally.

Gentle prompts

One short writing prompt at a time.

  1. Name one thing in the room with you right now. Write three ordinary details about it.

    Saved on this device only. Keep going, there is no wrong way to use this.

  2. Finish the sentence once, without fixing it: “Today I noticed…”

    Saved on this device only.

  3. Describe a sound you can hear, or the quiet itself, in a single short line.

    Saved on this device only.

  4. Write six words about something unfinished. Leave them unfinished if that fits.

    Saved on this device only.

  5. If a page could hold one small kindness for a later you, what would it say?

    Saved on this device only.

The Noticing Window

Look closely at one ordinary thing.

Look around you. Find something you normally don’t notice. Spend a minute looking at it.

  • What color is it?
  • What texture does it have?
  • What changed when you actually looked?

Saved on this device only.

The Tiny Drawing

Draw on paper, in about five lines.

Draw the shape of your mood without drawing a person, object, or symbol.

Or draw something using only five lines.

Paper is perfectly fine. There is no canvas here.

Describe this moment in six words. No more. No less. No explaining.

The Unfinished Thing

Start a sentence. Stopping is allowed.

Start a sentence. Stop wherever you want. You don’t have to finish it.

Quiet Shelf

A short list of things to notice. Pick one, or none.

Things you might notice. Not a checklist. Pick one, or none.

  • something blue
  • something older than you
  • something that makes a sound
  • something you have never really looked at
  • something that has survived
  • something soft
  • something that doesn’t need fixing
