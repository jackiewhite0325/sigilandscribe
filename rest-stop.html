Rest Stop

Every adventurer needs to rest. This is a cozy spot to recharge, with gentle things to do from right where you are.

🐾

Muffin wants you to know: rest is not quitting. Even the bravest adventurers take breaks. Your body is doing important work when you rest.

It is okay to...

You do not have to earn rest. These are all true, even on days when it does not feel like it.

💛

It is okay to rest even if you do not look sick.

🌧️

It is okay to miss out on fun things because your body needs a break.

🛋️

It is okay to spend the whole day on the couch or in bed.

😴

It is okay to feel tired even after sleeping a lot.

🤝

It is okay to ask for help, even with small things.

🌱

It is okay for rest to look different every day.

Gentle rest-day activities

These are made for low-energy days. Do one, do none, do them in any order. There is no wrong way to rest.

Imagination

Cloud Story

Look out a window or imagine the sky. Find a cloud and make up a story about it.

  1. Find a cloud shape (or picture one in your mind).
  2. What does it look like? An animal? A ship? A dragon?
  3. Where is it going? Who is riding on it?
  4. Tell the story out loud or just think it. Both count.
Listening

Sound Safari

Close your eyes and listen. How many different sounds can you find?

  1. Get cozy and close your eyes if you like.
  2. Listen for 1 minute. Count the sounds on your fingers.
  3. Can you find 5 sounds? 10?
  4. Pick your favorite sound. What makes it?
Breathing

Breathe with Muffin

Muffin breathes slow and deep when she rests. Try it with her.

Cozy

Five Senses Check-In

Notice what is around you right now. No need to change anything.

  • See: Name 3 things you can see from where you are.
  • Feel: What is the softest thing touching you right now?
  • Hear: What is the quietest sound around you?
  • Smell: Is there a smell nearby? Blanket? Fresh air?
  • Taste: Take a sip of water. Notice how it feels.
Gentle movement

Tiny Wiggles

Only if it feels good. Skip anything that hurts. You are in charge.

  1. Wiggle your toes. Just your toes. That counts.
  2. Roll your shoulders slowly, like a sleepy bear.
  3. Stretch your arms up like you are reaching for a star.
  4. Give yourself a hug. Squeeze gently.

If something hurts or feels wrong, stop. Rest is the activity.

Imagination

Muffin's Dream Adventure

Muffin is napping. Where do you think she is dreaming about?

  1. Close your eyes and picture Muffin asleep, paws twitching.
  2. Where is dream-Muffin? A beach? A mountain? Outer space?
  3. Who is with her? What is she chasing?
  4. When you open your eyes, you can draw it or just keep it in your head.

Remember: taking a rest day does not mean you are behind. It means you are listening to your body, and that is one of the bravest things an adventurer can do.
