Play Area · Early learning

Muffin's Corner

A play space for little ones ages 3 to 6, centered on Muffin. Short, friendly activities in the spirit of early learning toys: letters, numbers, bodies, feelings, and pretend, one small skill at a time.

Ages 3 to 6

Letters

Letter paws

Trace and name letters with Muffin as the guide. Coming as simple on-page play, not an app store download.

Numbers

Count with Muffin

Count treats, steps, and wiggles up to ten. Built for short attention and big grins.

Body

Wiggle break

Move like Muffin: stretch, freeze, shake the wiggles out. Gross-motor play for living rooms.

Feelings

How does Muffin feel?

Name happy, tired, brave, and still. Soft vocabulary for hard days, sized for preschool hearts.

Pretend

Vet clinic

Care for a stuffed friend the way Muffin's people cared for her. Imagination first, no screens required.

Listen

Story time

Short read-aloud moments tied to the series. Links to Book 1 when you want the full story on the shelf.

Not the series storefront. Books and the St. Jude donation commitment live on the Muffin the Pitbull Series page in the Library. This corner is play and early learning only.

Activities above are planned stations. Nothing is sold or unlocked here. When a station is ready, the card will open.
