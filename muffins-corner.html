Muffin’s Corner

A free playroom for little ones. Explore Muffin’s world, tap a place to play, and try letters, treats, wiggles, feelings, pretend, and stories.

Ages 3 to 7 · Free

Woof! Hi! I’m Muffin. Come explore my world below. You can play as long as you like. If you need a rest, that is okay too.

Tap me to turn sound on or off

Muffin’s world: tap a place to visit

Swipe to look around, then tap a place.

Letter paws

Tap a letter. Hear its name. Say it out loud with Muffin.

Count the treats

Add a treat or take one away. Count out loud up to ten.

0

Wiggle break

Pick a move. Do it with your body. Grown-up can join.

How does Muffin feel?

Tap a feeling. You can feel that way too. All feelings are welcome here.

Vet clinic

Use a stuffed friend, a doll, or your hands. Follow the steps. No screen needed after you read them.

  1. 1. Soft voice: “Hello, friend. I am the helper today.”
  2. 2. Gentle check: ears, paws, tummy, one at a time.
  3. 3. A pretend bandage or a soft blanket.
  4. 4. Water and rest. “You are safe.”
  5. 5. A thank-you hug or high-five.

Care is a kind of courage.

Story time

A tiny story you can hear out loud. Press for another.

Muffin: Once there was a small gray dog who loved pillows and people. When the wiggles came, her family stayed close. That was enough.

For grown-ups

This corner is free play only. It is not a book shop and not a catalogue. Series books and the donation commitment live on the Library series page.

Donation language: 5% of net series royalties from the Muffin the Pitbull™ Series are donated every quarter to St. Jude Children's Research Hospital in Muffin's name. Update checkpoints: December 20, March 20, June 20, September 20. No dollar totals are shown here.

Newsletter (grown-up email signup, not for children to fill in alone): Muffin Newsletter.

Nothing here asks a child for a name, email, photo, or location. Sound uses recorded read-aloud files and only starts when a child taps.
