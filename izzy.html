What a keepsake is

A keepsake is a short picture book about the one you love, built around the moments only you know. The way they greeted you at the door. The spot on the couch that stayed theirs. The last good day.

Grief comes in many shapes: a child, a partner, a parent, a friend, a pet, or the comfort object that carried someone through the hard days. A keepsake holds it, whatever shape it takes.

One careful note: we are bookmakers, not counselors. A keepsake can hold a story, but it is not grief counseling, therapy, or crisis support.

Where keepsakes live now

Custom keepsakes have moved off the website and onto Lulu, where each one gets its own quiet page and the printing and shipping are handled there. When the first keepsake is ready, its link will be right here.
