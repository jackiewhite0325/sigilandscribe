How the door works

  1. Send the form. Your dog, your moments, your words. It costs nothing.
  2. I read it and come back with a plan and one price.
  3. You pay through one link, made for your order. It works once.
  4. We revise until it reads true. The finished PDF arrives by email. If you want a print copy for the shelf, that is offered after you approve the digital book.

Why there is no buy button

No buy button on purpose. Every order starts with your story, not your card. Money moves only when the plan is agreed, and every payment link is single use, made for one order, so no one can pay without a story attached.
