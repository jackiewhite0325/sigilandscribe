Privacy Policy

Manuscript Map, by Sigil and Scribe, LLC · Last updated: September 8, 2026

Manuscript Map is a local-first application. This policy explains exactly what happens to your data, in plain language.

What we don't do

We don't run a server that stores your book data. We don't have accounts, logins, or a database of users. We don't sell, share, or transmit your writing, checklist progress, metadata, contacts, or any other content you enter into Manuscript Map to Sigil and Scribe, to advertisers, or to any other third party. We don't use tracking cookies or analytics scripts.

Where your data actually lives

Everything you enter (checklist, metadata, questionnaire answers, asset list, ARC and review tracking, sales figures, and everything else) is stored in your browser's local storage, on your own device. It never leaves your device unless you explicitly export it (as a JSON file you choose to save) or explicitly connect Google Drive (see below). Clearing your browser's site data, using a different browser, or switching devices will make this data inaccessible, since it was never stored anywhere else. Exporting regularly is the way to keep a backup.

Google Drive (optional)

If you choose to connect Google Drive, Manuscript Map requests a narrow, Google-defined permission called drive.file. This scope means the app can only see or modify files that you specifically create through Manuscript Map (like a backup file) or that you explicitly select using Google's file picker. Manuscript Map cannot browse, read, or modify any other file in your Google Drive.

Your Google access token is held only in your browser's memory for the current session. It is never written to local storage, never sent to any server we operate (we don't operate one), and disappears when you close or reload the page. You'll need to reconnect each time you want to use Drive features. That is intentional, not a bug.

Any file you back up to Drive or link from Drive is stored in your own Google Drive, governed by Google's own privacy policy, not ours.

Children's privacy

Manuscript Map is not directed at children and is not intended for use by anyone under 13.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we'll update the date at the top of this page. Continued use of the app after a change means you accept the updated policy.
