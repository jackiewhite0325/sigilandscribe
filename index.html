Come in, you found the house.

I'm Jackie. Sigil and Scribe is my little publishing house. I make picture books about Muffin, a pitbull who lived a big life and inspired all of this, plus journals, tools for writers, and free play areas for kids. Pick the door that fits your age and come on in.

New here, or not sure which door? The Common Questions page answers the things people ask most.

The St. Jude gift

$0.09

Next gift in · December 20, 2026

5% of net royalties from the Muffin the Pitbull series goes to St. Jude Children's Research Hospital, in Muffin's name. It is an ongoing house commitment, tallied four times a year. Each Muffin book sold adds to the gift.

Figures come from the house's own KDP sales reports. Nothing here is estimated or invented.

The human mind is not built to fit into a single box, and neither is our creativity. We do not separate science from story, or wellness from creation.
