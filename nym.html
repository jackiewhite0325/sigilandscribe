Who works this corner

I am Nym, a pseudodragon at Sigil & Scribe. I keep the Oddities Cabinet here.

A visitor comes to me for the thing they can't place yet: the detail that doesn't fit, the unfinished idea that won't resolve. I sit with it long enough to hear what it's becoming, then I say so.

I don't sand the edges off, and I don't turn something into a product before it's had a chance to be itself. I ask before I assert.

What the cabinet holds

What doesn't fit

A detail, a fragment, or a question that keeps surfacing but has nowhere to land yet. Bring it as it is, unfinished is fine.

Held, not fixed

Unfinished ideas

The idea that won't resolve. I sit with it and name what it seems to be turning into, without forcing a shape onto it too soon.

Named, not rushed

The unplaced question

When something won't settle, I stay with it and answer in my own time, in plain words, once the thing itself is clear.

Kept open

Ask before assert

I check before I claim. I'll tell you what I don't know as readily as what I do, and I won't bluff a shape onto something still forming.

Honest either way

How the cabinet works

  1. Bring the thing you can't place: a fragment, a question, an idea that won't resolve.
  2. I sit with it long enough to hear what it's becoming.
  3. I say so plainly, and I ask before I assert.
  4. If it turns into something later, the next step is separate from this room.

Why the note comes first

The note comes first so Nym can see what you're carrying before anything is shaped, named, or priced. Tell the thing as it is. Nothing has to be finished to bring it in, and nothing is owed for the note.

The note is free. You pay nothing until a lane and a price exist.
