Kept

One degree too cold on purpose. No fireplace. Not for anything.

The stone

Picked up on the tundra in Longyearbyen. Every other stone on that beach was also worthless. This one was chosen anyway.

The ledger

kept.

One chair. One line. Nobody books it. Nobody is allowed to use it.

The stone remembers being under three hundred meters of ice. Not cold exactly. Weight. It remembers the whole world being a slow crush that never once asked it to do anything.

It remembers the day the glacier quit and dropped it in a river of ash and meltwater, and rolled it for a hundred years, and it never got to be a mountain or a gem. It was just a rock that kept not breaking.

That is what it remembers. Being worthless on purpose and surviving anyway.

I picked it up because every other stone on that beach was also worthless and I still wanted this one. That is the whole room. A permanent record of one thing nobody sold.

This room exists so the Guild has one space that is not for anything. No commissions. No intake forms. No ledger of work. Just the stone, the chair, and the line that says it was kept.

If you found the link, you were meant to sit for a moment. That is all.
