Privacy Policy

Last Updated: September 28, 2026

Sigil and Scribe, LLC ("we," "us," or "our") operates the website at https://jackiewhite0325.github.io/sigilandscribe (the "site"). This policy explains what information the site collects, what it does not collect, and who handles your information when you use our forms or links.

1. WHAT WE COLLECT

Information you give us directly:

  • Newsletter signups. The Muffin Newsletter and the Sigil and Scribe Newsletter forms ask for your email address. What you type goes to Tally (our form provider) and into our Google Sheets, and is used only to send you the newsletter you asked for.
  • Guild and project forms. The Artisan Guildhall application and the Project Submission Form ask for whatever the form requests, such as your name, email, and details about your project. This goes to Tally and into our Google Sheets, and is used only to review and respond to your submission.
  • Email. If you write to us at jackiewhite0325@gmail.com or to one of our house addresses, we have your email address and whatever you wrote. We use it only to answer you.

Information collected automatically:

  • Google Analytics runs on every page. It collects anonymous visit data such as pages viewed, browser type, and approximate location. We use it to understand traffic and improve the site. We do not use it to identify you.
  • Google Fonts loads from Google's servers when pages display, which means your browser makes a request to Google. This is standard for web fonts.

2. TOOLS THAT RUN IN YOUR BROWSER

Manuscript Map and the journal prompts tool store your work locally in your browser. There is no account, no sign-in, and nothing is sent to us. Export or print saves a file to your own device, which is the only backup path. Clearing your browser data may erase what is stored there, so export anything you want to keep.

3. THIRD PARTIES WHO HANDLE INFORMATION

  • Tally (tally.so): hosts our forms. Their privacy policy applies to what you type into a form.
  • Google Sheets: stores form responses for our newsletters and submissions.
  • Google Analytics and Google Fonts: described above.
  • Ko-fi (ko-fi.com): handles donations. If you donate, Ko-fi processes it under their own policies; we do not see your payment details.
  • Amazon: book purchases happen on amazon.com. Amazon handles payment and delivery under their own policies. Our links include an affiliate tag, which means we may earn a small commission if you buy through them.

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information. We share it only with the providers above, as needed to operate the site and answer you.

4. WHAT WE DO NOT DO

  • No accounts. The site has no user accounts, no logins, and no checkout. We do not process payments on the site.
  • No health data. The Zen Den and hypnotherapy content is educational and literary, for general relaxation. We are not a medical provider, and nothing here is covered by HIPAA. We do not ask for, collect, or want your medical records, diagnoses, or health history. Please do not send them through our forms or email.
  • No Mailchimp. We do not use Mailchimp or any email marketing platform beyond the newsletter forms described above.

5. HOW WE USE YOUR INFORMATION

  • To send the newsletters you signed up for (unsubscribe anytime via the link in any email, or by writing to us).
  • To review and respond to guild and project submissions.
  • To answer your emails.
  • To understand site traffic through anonymous analytics.
  • To comply with the law, if required by court order or to protect the rights and safety of the house and its visitors.

6. CHILDREN'S PRIVACY

Muffin's Corner is a free playroom for ages 3 to 7. It collects nothing: no names, no emails, no photos, no locations. Our forms and newsletters are for grown-ups. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has submitted personal details to us, contact us immediately at jackiewhite0325@gmail.com and we will remove it.

7. YOUR RIGHTS

Depending on where you live, you may have rights over your personal information (such as access, deletion, or opt-out under laws like the CCPA or GDPR). To exercise any of these, write to jackiewhite0325@gmail.com with a clear request and we will respond.

8. CHANGES TO THIS POLICY

We may update this policy as the site changes. When we do, we will update the "Last Updated" date above. Continued use of the site after a change means you accept the updated policy.
