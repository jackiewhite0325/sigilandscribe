Who works this corner

I am John. I run a working place: animals, plants, property, and the money that holds them. None of it is a hobby. This corner sells the systems that keep it running on schedule, and they are the same systems the place actually runs on.

Every sheet started as a chore that slipped and a fix that held. The watering rotation that ended skipped plants. The feed log that caught a low bag before the store run became an emergency. The ledger that does not let a month lie to you.

The standard is simple. If it would not survive a busy season and a wet winter on a real place, it does not leave this corner.
