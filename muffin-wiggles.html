Muffin Gets the Wiggles

Book 1 of the Muffin the Pitbull™ Series.

Muffin is a small gray pitbull with the wiggles: her legs dance without her permission, and pillows become landing pads. When a seizure scrambles her afternoon, her family stays close, tug-of-war waits, and Muffin learns that asking for help is brave. Book 1 of the Muffin the Pitbull series. 5% of net series royalties are donated quarterly to St. Jude Children's Research Hospital.
