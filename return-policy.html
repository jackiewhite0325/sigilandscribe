Return & Refund Policy

Sigil and Scribe, LLC · Last updated: September 25, 2026

Manuscript Map

Manuscript Map has no paid unlock, Pro tier, subscription, or in-app purchase. All features (Questionnaire, Checklist, Metadata Vault, Asset Tracker, ARC Tracker, Review Log, Promo Submission Tracker, Launch Calendar, Distribution Tracker, Sales & Royalty Log, Ad Spend & ROI Tracker) are available in your browser. Because nothing is sold through Manuscript Map, there are no refunds to process for this tool.

Books and other digital products

Books purchased through Amazon or other retailers follow that retailer’s return and refund policies. Digital downloads or commissions sold directly by Sigil and Scribe are generally non-refundable once delivered, except where required by law or where the file is defective. Contact us within 14 days if a delivered file is unusable and we will replace it or refund as appropriate.

Custom commissions (Keepsakes, etc.)

Custom work is quoted before any payment is taken. Once a plan is accepted and work begins, deposits are non-refundable. Final delivery follows the agreed plan. Questions about a specific commission should be raised with the house.
