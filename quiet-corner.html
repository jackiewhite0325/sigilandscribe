A quiet corner

This corner is open, for whatever reason brought you here.

Maybe you are carrying something heavy. Maybe you just want to say hello, tell me what a book meant to you, or ask a question that does not fit anywhere else. All of that belongs here.

Write to me. I read every note myself, and I answer as one person, not a company.

One honest note: I am a bookmaker, not a counselor, and a note here is not crisis support. If you are in crisis right now, the footer of this page has the numbers that answer fast.
