About J. White

J. White writes books for readers who need warmth, humor, and a little truth. The Muffin Series, a 26-book picture book journey, began as a memorial to her rescued pitbull Muffin, who taught her family that brave comes in small, wiggly bodies. She also writes humor and survival guides for the chronic illness community, quote collections for the sharp-tongued, and coloring books for anyone who needs to slow down.

J. White publishes under Sigil and Scribe, LLC, and gives a portion of Muffin Series proceeds to St. Jude Children's Research Hospital in Muffin's name.

Petra C.Ht.

Petra C.Ht. is a Certified Hypnotherapist and an Honors Graduate of the Hypnosis Motivation Institute in Tarzana, California, the only nationally accredited college of hypnotherapy in the United States. She trained in Clinical, Ericksonian, and Kappasinian Hypnotherapy, and holds a Bachelor of Science in Business Management, earned Summa Cum Laude.

Her practice areas include tinnitus habituation, insomnia and anxiety support, academic acceleration, and chronic illness support. At Sigil and Scribe she curates the Subconscious Shelf: guided neuroregulation audio, acoustic tracks, and the gaze anchors that anchor the study. Everything on the shelf is educational and relaxation focused, never a substitute for professional care.
