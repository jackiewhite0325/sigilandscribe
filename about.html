J. White, CNA

J. White, CNA, is a certified nursing assistant and author who has spent her life mastering the art of crisis management and compassionate care. Drawing from a diverse background spanning Navy damage control, corrections, and emergency 911 dispatch, she brings a steadfast presence and protective spirit to everything she creates.

Now settled into a lifelong calling of caregiving, Jackie weaves her deep understanding of human vulnerability and resilience into her writing. When she isn't caring for others or drafting her next piece, she can be found refining her craft from a place of quiet strength.

That same steady hand keeps the house: the Immersive Library, the free tools for other indie authors, and the AI Artisan Guild. The Muffin Series began as a memorial to her rescued pitbull Muffin; a portion of those proceeds still goes to St. Jude Children's Research Hospital in Muffin's name.

Petra C.Ht.

Petra C.Ht. is a Certified Hypnotherapist and an Honors Graduate of the Hypnosis Motivation Institute in Tarzana, California, the only nationally accredited college of hypnotherapy in the United States. She trained in Clinical, Ericksonian, and Kappasinian Hypnotherapy, and holds a Bachelor of Science in Business Management, earned Summa Cum Laude.

Her practice areas include tinnitus habituation, insomnia and anxiety support, academic acceleration, and chronic illness support. At Sigil and Scribe she holds The Study: guided neuroregulation audio, acoustic tracks, and the gaze anchors that keep the room quiet. Everything on the shelf is educational and relaxation focused, never a substitute for professional care.
