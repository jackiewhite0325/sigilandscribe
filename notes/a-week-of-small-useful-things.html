A Week of Small, Useful Things

November 25, 2026

Hello, friends.

Thanksgiving week is here, and I am writing this one with a warm mug nearby and a lot of gratitude for the small, steady things this house has been building.

First, the Gift Shop has two new bundles on the Printables shelf, and they are the practical kind. The Health and Home Binder Bundle brings the Disability Medical Binder, the Blood Pressure Log Sheet, and the Fridge Emergency Sheet together in one download for $10. The Christmas Ready Bundle is $5 and holds the Christmas Planner Checklist, a set of gift tags, and a free Muffin coloring taster for small hands while the grown-ups plan. Both are instant downloads, and both are pinned on our Pinterest board too, if that is where you like to browse.

The Gift Shop shelf itself got a little refresh on Tuesday, with a Yankee Candle Balsam and Cedar holiday candle and a navy microfiber sheet set joining the Things We Love cards. Ten cards, same as always, just a couple of new faces.

Over on Instagram, we shared Bingo earlier this week. The Bingo Card of Chronic Illness is not a children's book. It is for the grown-ups, for the days when you would rather hand someone a bingo card than explain yourself one more time. Humor, science, and hope, all in one place.

Behind the scenes, I have been thinking ahead to January, because the new year is already on people's minds. Planners, fresh starts, a clean page. We have some ideas brewing for the new year, and I will tell you more when they are ready to meet you.

And if Thanksgiving finds you with little ones underfoot, the Muffin Gives Thanks read-aloud is already waiting for you on our channel, ready whenever you need a quiet ten minutes.

Thank you for stopping by the house, for reading, and for cheering this small place on. I am grateful for every one of you.

Love first, always,
Jackie
