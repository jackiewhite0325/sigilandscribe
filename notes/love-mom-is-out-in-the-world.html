Love, Mom Is Out in the World

November 11, 2026

Love, Mom is out today. The paperback is $16.99 and the Kindle edition is $9.99, and both went live this morning. This book is a set of letters written as a journal on life, with guidance that comes with a hug attached, and I am glad it gets to meet its readers at last. The same day, Chapter 2 of When the Dice Roll arrives on the teen shelf, the next piece of our weekly serial. One chapter a week, as we write it. Two releases sharing one day felt like a lot when I planned it, and it still does. But they are both ready, and I am glad they get to share the day.

The site got a tune-up

This week I ran a full health check across the whole site, all 113 pages, and every one of them answered back. The check found a few broken links and some missing descriptions, and we fixed them all: dead memorial links now point to the About page, every page carries a proper description, and the pages share cleanly when you send them to a friend. I also closed out the last of the Spanish translation choices, so the Spanish set is finished and live. A tidy site is a kind site, and I want every door here to open the way it should.

The shelf and the inbox

Our Things We Love shelf in the Gift Shop now holds ten cards, and it is learning to listen: each morning it looks at what people are actually buying, inside the categories I already chose, and offers new leads from those lists. Nothing lands on the shelf without passing the same checks as always. Closer to home, I cleared out more than two thousand old promotional emails this week, and my inbox went from crowded to calm. Room to breathe helps everything else work better.

A small honest project

My domain name, sigilandscribe.com, slipped away when a payment lapsed, and I have until about November 20 to reclaim it before it is gone for good. I decided to earn the renewal money from things already sitting on my shelves: a lot of old South American banknotes, a sealed nickel set, a few sports cards, and six copies of a little play called Bachelor of the Year. The first piece, the Bachelor of the Year set, is listed on eBay now, and nothing goes live without my say so. It is a small pile, but it is mine, and it might just buy the house its name back.

A word of love

Thank you for reading, for listening, and for letting this house grow one careful piece at a time. Showing up counts, and so does rest. I hope your week holds a cozy chair, a warm mug, and a story that asks nothing of you.

With love,
Jackie
