House Notes

Book 2 Releases Tomorrow

October 30, 2026

Muffin Gets Tired a Lot comes out tomorrow, Saturday, October 31. I checked the store earlier this week, and both the paperback and the ebook are cleared and ready for release day. If you have a tired kid in your life, or if you are a tired grown-up who remembers being one, this one is for you. Muffin gets tired a lot, and that is allowed here.

Love, Mom is in

Love, Mom, my journal on life, is submitted to KDP in both formats: paperback at $16.99 and Kindle at $9.99. Both are scheduled for Veterans Day, November 11. The book is 40 letters, and it is the one I am proudest of so far. It is in KDP review now, and author copies come next. I will share more as release day gets closer.

The Gift Shop got easier to browse

Two changes went live in the Gift Shop this week. First, there is a "Things We Love" shelf: nine picks, each one checked by hand, with a real photo, no prices on the cards, and a clear disclosure on the box. Second, the shop sections themselves are now carousels instead of one long scroll, so you are not scrolling forever downward to see everything. Browsing on a phone should feel much kinder now.

I also want to say thank you plainly: the shop had its first small affiliate sale this week. It is a tiny amount, and it matters to me, because it means someone found something useful through this little house. Thank you.

Behind the scenes

Two quieter pieces of work landed too. Muffin Off Leash, the teen space, got a fresh-eyes pass: the hub now carries a 988 support block, the pages are back in our house fonts, and a few wordings are softer and truer to how we want to talk to teens. And the teen novel I am building, When the Dice Roll, has a title I love, a full 30 chapter outline, and its first two chapters tidied up and ready for my own approval read. More on that one when it is ready to meet readers.

I also closed a door this week, gently and on purpose. The Trinity Tension Saga is archived, not deleted, and its two site pages now carry short retirement notes. Some stories are not the right fit for this house, and putting them to rest with care is part of the work too.

Coming next week

I am shaping our first newsletter for the week of November 2, with more business and less personal news, and free PDFs tucked in for readers. Nothing sends until I have given it my final go.

Thank you for being here, for reading, and for letting this house grow slowly and honestly. Showing up counts, and so does rest.

With love,
Jackie
