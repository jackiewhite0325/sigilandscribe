Five Days Out

October 26, 2026

Five days out

Muffin Gets Tired a Lot comes out this Saturday, October 31. That is five days away, and I can feel it in the best way: a little flutter, a little tired, a lot of love for this small tired dog and the kids she was made for.

Today is our details lock, before the release check on Wednesday. I already fixed one small thing I am glad I caught: the book's reading age was missing from its store page, so I added it.

The quiet work behind the house

Last week was not flashy, and I am proud of it anyway. I checked our company record myself, and Sigil and Scribe is Active and In Good Standing.

None of that is a storybook. All of it is how a little house stays honest and safe enough for people to trust.

November is ready, too. Our social posts for the month are written and scheduled, with the first newsletter coming in the week of November 2.

Punkin, week two

Punkin started her second week of training with her very first sit session: five sits in about two minutes. That was also the first time I used treats with her, after a whole treat-free first week of praise and play. She stared up at me afterwards, asking for more, and I understood completely.

Her awake windows are getting longer now, two to two and a half hours, sometimes three, and she spends most of them wanting to play, play, play. She followed me all the way to the little library building and back the other day, right beside the wagon, with Rosie and Zero alongside.

I also sent a thank you card to the woman who gave Punkin to us, with a photo of her curled up in her blankets. She has been a gift.

A word of love

Some weeks, showing up is the whole thing. I have had hard nights and slow mornings lately, and I am learning again that rest is not quitting. If you are tired today, you still count. You do not have to earn a soft place to land.

Thank you for being here, however you found us. You are welcome at the house.

With love,
Jackie
