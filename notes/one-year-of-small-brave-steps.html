One Year of Small Brave Steps

October 27, 2026

One year ago, Sigil and Scribe became a real company. Five books, a whole website, and a lot of small brave steps later, it is still here, and so am I.

The five books: The Many Faces of Grace. The Bingo Card of Chronic Illness. Don’t Quote Me. And Muffin the Pitbull, who arrived with Muffin Gets the Wiggles and has a brand new book, Muffin Gets Tired a Lot, coming this Saturday.

The website grew rooms for children, for teens, and for grown-ups, and learned to say hello in four languages. None of it was built fast, and none of it was built alone. Readers, friends, and one small puppy currently in training have all carried pieces of this year.

Thank you for being part of the first year. Here is to the second, taken at the same pace as the first: love first, always, one small brave step at a time.

With love,
Jackie
