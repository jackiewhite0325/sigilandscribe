All Somebody's Words

October 10, 2026

This week is Banned Books Week, and I want to say plainly why it matters in this house.

I collect books to keep them available. Some of the books on my shelves have been challenged, questioned, or pushed to the side because somebody decided they were too much, too honest, or too uncomfortable for other people to read.

I do not have to agree with every word in a book to believe the book should stay within reach. A story that is not for me might be the exact story somebody else needs to feel less alone. A hard book might be the one that finally gives a reader words for their own life.

That is why my reading shelves are staying wide open. You can wander them here: Books to Read Before You Die.

They are all somebody's words and they are all valuable.

If a book has ever found you at the right time, you already understand. Keep reading. Keep sharing. Keep the shelves open for the next person who needs them.
