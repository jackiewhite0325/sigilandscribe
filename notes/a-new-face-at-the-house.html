A New Face at the House

October 19, 2026

A new face at the house

I did not plan to come home from the flea market with a puppy. I tried saying no, and I lost. So this week the house gained a new face: Punkin, a seven and a half week old mini Queensland, black and tan, who has already stolen the whole pack. Zero has stepped down from service dog to ESA (he is just too big to take most places), and Cindy looked at me and said I needed a new service dog. So Punkin will be my service dog in training. We start with the tiniest steps: her name, her potty spot, the crate being a good place. One to three minutes at a time. She is a heel nipper, classic heeler herding nip, so we redirect with chew toys. And she is already doing the job in the way that matters most. The day she came home, she made me forget about my pain for a while. That is a service dog at work, right there.

Opening the door wider

I realized most of the people visiting the site right now are in China. That changes things. Google Fonts does not work there, and neither does Google Analytics. So we fixed it the honest way. All the fonts now live on our own site, all seventy pages, and we took the analytics tag off completely. Pages load faster, and nobody's typeface depends on a company that blocks half the map.

And then we got ambitious. The site now speaks four ways: English, Simplified Chinese, Traditional Chinese, and Spanish. Thirteen pages in Simplified, thirteen in Traditional, and Spanish rolling along behind them. I do not read Chinese or Spanish well enough to check the translations myself, so every page got a second look from another AI, and the Chinese pages carry a note saying we made them with love, we are not native speakers, and if you find an error, write to me and help us fix it. Love first means meeting people where they are, even when we do it a little clumsily. Reader feedback will make it better over time.

We also fixed the grown-ups' corner. The Cafe landing page was just a title and a picture, and nobody could find the good stuff inside. Now it opens like a door should, with three rooms and a note pointing lost visitors to the Common Questions. Links look like links again, the 404 page points to real doors, and Google gets the right language tags for every page.

Twelve days out

Muffin Gets Tired a Lot comes out October 31. Twelve days from now. The paperback and the ebook are uploaded and scheduled. That is the second Muffin book, the one about being tired a lot and learning that tired is not the same as broken. Our St. Jude gift meter sits at nine cents, built on the first sales to come. Every book gives.

Thank you for being here, however you found us. You are welcome at the house.

With love,
Jackie
