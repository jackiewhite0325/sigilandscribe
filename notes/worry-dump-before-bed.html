Worry Dump Pages Before Bed

October 3, 2026

Here is the whole technique: before bed, take one page and write down everything on your mind. Not neatly. Not in order. Just out.

The worries that keep you awake are usually not big, clear problems. They are small, blurry ones, and your brain keeps spinning them because it is afraid you will forget them by morning. Writing them down tells your brain the job is handled. It can stand down.

Try this:

1. Set a timer for five minutes.
2. Write every worry, task, and loose end that surfaces. One line each is fine.
3. Star the ones that actually need you tomorrow. Let the rest stay on the page.
4. Close the notebook. The page keeps them now, not you.

Researchers call this offloading. The rest of us call it sleeping better. It works best when it becomes boring, a small nightly ritual like locking the front door.

We made a printable worry dump page for the Free Things shelf, so you can start tonight. One page, five minutes, then rest.
