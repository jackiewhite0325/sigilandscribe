Looking Toward January

November 27, 2026

Hello, friends.

The turkey is carved, the pie is a happy memory, and around here the very next thought is January. Not the loud, resolution kind of January. The quiet kind: a clean page, a calm shelf, and one tidy place for the papers that matter.

So the Gift Shop is ready for it. The January 2027 calendar page is free on the Printables shelf, one page to print and plan gently with. Beside it sits the Health and Home Binder Bundle, my Disability Medical Binder with its Blood Pressure Log Sheet and Fridge Emergency Sheet in one download, for anyone who wants their health papers in one calm place before the new year turns. We pinned the bundle on Pinterest this morning too, for the January planners already gathering ideas.

The Things We Love shelf had its small weekly refresh today: a clear shower curtain liner joined the cards, and the lounge set waved goodbye. Ten cards, same as always.

And one date to keep in your pocket: Muffin Goes to the Hospital, Book 3 in Muffin's series, arrives early in the new year. More on that as the day gets closer.

Thank you for spending a little of your holiday week with this small house. I hope the weekend ahead of you is gentle.

Love first, always,
Jackie
