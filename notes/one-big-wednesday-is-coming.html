One Big Wednesday Is Coming

November 6, 2026

Next Wednesday, November 11, is a double day here at the house. Love, Mom comes out, 40 letters written as a journal on life, with guidance that comes with a hug attached. The paperback will be $16.99 and the Kindle edition $9.99, and both are ready and waiting. The same day, Chapter 2 of When the Dice Roll arrives on the teen shelf, the next piece of our weekly serial. One chapter a week, as we write it. Chapter 1 is already live, and it feels good to see the teen stories shelf no longer empty.

Every word on the site, checked

This week the site's read-aloud audio got a careful going over, every lesson, story, and button in Muffin's Corner, listened to word by word. A few small things were found and fixed, including one story that had no voice at all and now does. Small fixes, but they matter to me. A house that says it cares about the details should sound like it.

A word of love

Thank you for reading, for listening, and for letting this house grow one careful piece at a time. Showing up counts, and so does rest. I hope your weekend holds a cozy chair, a warm mug, and a story that asks nothing of you.

With love,
Jackie
