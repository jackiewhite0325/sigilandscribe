House Notes

Muffin Gives Thanks Is Out in the World

November 4, 2026

Muffin Gives Thanks is live. It is our Thanksgiving special, a paperback on Amazon for $9.99, and it is also free to watch as a read-aloud on our Muffin's Corner YouTube channel. That pairing is how our specials work: a paperback you can hold, or a free story you can watch, never an ebook.

The read-aloud is read in a warm voice, with each spread on screen, and it was a joy to send it to family and friends yesterday. One friend wrote back that it made her cry, in the good way, and that she is thankful for me. That is the whole reason this house exists. A story that reaches one person kindly is enough.

You can find a "Story time, on us" section on our homepage now, linking to the channel, and a Muffin Gives Thanks card in the Gift Shop with buttons to watch or buy.

The shop grew, and the Gift Shop got tidier

Our Lulu shop now holds 22 products. Eleven journals got new covers this week, each with its own plant, from wildflowers to ferns. The shop also gained Muffin's Christmas Coloring and Activity Book and A Year at the Book House, our 2027 wall calendar, so the coming-soon panel is retired and the shelf now says "Fresh in the shop."

The Gift Shop got some honest tidying too. The Wiggles and Tired a Lot cards have their Buy on Amazon buttons back, with real prices, and the series page now says Books 1 and 2 are available, because they are. Every Lulu book card now carries a "Peek inside" link with sample pages, since Lulu itself only offers a preview for the calendar.

A deck, a game, and one week to Love, Mom

Behind the scenes, I opened a Sigil and Scribe account at The Game Crafter, a print-on-demand home for card decks and board games. Our 78 Reflections deck already lives free on the site, and its physical form is now being built, one piece at a time. Nothing goes public until I have held the proof in my hands. My board game, Energy Bones, reached its playtest edition this week too. Slow builds are welcome here.

And one week from today, on Veterans Day, November 11, Love, Mom comes out: 40 letters, written as a journal on life, with guidance that comes with a hug attached. Use what fits, and skip what does not. The paperback will be $16.99 and the Kindle edition $9.99, and the Gift Shop card now carries a pre-order button.

A word of love

Thanksgiving is close, and gratitude is on my mind. Thank you for reading, for stopping by, and for letting this house grow at its own pace. Showing up counts, and so does rest.

With love,
Jackie
