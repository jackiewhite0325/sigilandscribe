Ten Days Out

October 21, 2026

Ten days out

Muffin Gets Tired a Lot arrives on October 31, ten days from now. Yesterday the pre-order post went up on my Instagram: paperback and ebook both coming Halloween day. It is the second Muffin book, the one about being tired all the time and learning that tired is not the same as broken.

A small shop note, since honesty is the house rule here: sales are slower than I hoped they would be by now. They are not zero, though, and I am grateful for every single one. Slow is not the same as stopped.

And this week I got to give something back, too. I wrote out what helps me live with degenerative disc disease and posted it in my support group this morning: everything I have learned, in my own words, so nobody there has to start from nothing. What works for me, and why, and a reminder that rest counts and so do you. That is the whole house philosophy in one post. Love first, always.

The plumbing of being found

My marketing plan has always been love first, great SEO second. The love part I can do. The SEO part needed real plumbing, so this week we put it in.

It turns out I had set up Google Search Console myself back on October 9 and completely forgotten (my brain is not a filing cabinet). We finished what past-me started: the homepage is indexed on Google, a fresh sitemap lists all 105 live pages, and traffic counts come from Cloudflare now. No cookies, no identifying anyone, just totals. And the kids' and teen pages are excluded entirely, because my own rule says no tracking where the small ones play.

I also made one honesty fix I am proud of. The About and Series pages used to just say 26 Muffin books, as if they all existed, while the shop only carries two. Now they say planned, in all four languages. The dream is real and 26 books long. But two of them are the ones you can actually hold right now, and that is worth saying straight.

Four days of Punkin

Day four of puppy school, and Punkin walks herself to her potty spot now. No carrying, no coaxing, she just goes. She is weaning onto hard kibble and choosing it over the soft stuff, which tells me her little jaw is ready. She still makes me laugh harder than I can remember laughing, my tiny Tasmanian devil menace. Name games and gentle handling practice this week, a few minutes at a time. She is already doing the job in the biggest way, just by being here and being funny.

Thank you for being here, however you found us. You are welcome at the house.

With love,
Jackie
