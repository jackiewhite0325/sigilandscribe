House Notes

Launch week

October 1, 2026

The books are live. The Bingo Card of Chronic Illness is on Amazon in ebook and paperback, and The Many Faces of Grace is out as an ebook. The Grace paperback follows on Saturday.

The website got a polish pass too. The Quiet Room has a breathing light now, dim lighting, and optional writing spaces that save to your device. The homepage's honesty line changed with it: the agents stepped out of the messaging picture, and Pearl stayed on as the house's friendly helper. Every page on the site survived a link audit without a single dead end.

This is House Notes, by the way. Company news, launches, and whatever else needs saying, all in one room. The rest of the house stays quiet.

More soon. There is always more soon.
