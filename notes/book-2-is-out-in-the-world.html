House Notes

Book 2 Is Out in the World

November 2, 2026

Muffin Gets Tired a Lot came out on Saturday, October 31, and it is now live as a paperback and an ebook. If you have a tired kid in your life, or if you are a tired grown-up who remembers being one, this book is for you. Muffin gets tired a lot here, and that is allowed. Thank you to everyone who cheered this little book on its way. Seeing it out in the world still feels a bit unreal to me, in the best way.

Love, Mom is next

My next book, Love, Mom, comes out on Veterans Day, November 11. It is 40 letters, written as a journal on life, with guidance that comes with a hug attached: use what fits, and skip what does not. The paperback will be $16.99 and the Kindle edition $9.99. I am proudest of this one so far, and I will share more as its release day gets closer.

The Gift Shop, and a small honest number

The Gift Shop's "Things We Love" shelf is holding steady at nine picks, each one checked by hand, with a real photo and a clear disclosure on the box, and no prices on the cards. The shop sections are still in their carousel form too, so browsing should feel kinder on a phone than one long scroll.

I also have a small, honest number to share. In the last seven days, this little site welcomed 11 visits and 12 page views. That is small, and I love it anyway. It means real people stopped by this house, and that is the whole point. We are growing slowly, and slowly is fine here.

Shaping our first newsletter

This week I am shaping our first newsletter, with more business news and less personal news, and free PDFs tucked in for readers. It will not send until I have given it my final go, so nothing is rushing out the door. If you would like to hear from this house once a month, the sign-up is on the site, and I would be glad to have you along.

Thank you for reading, for stopping by, and for letting this house grow at its own pace. Showing up counts, and so does rest. I am grateful for both, in you and in me.

With love,
Jackie
