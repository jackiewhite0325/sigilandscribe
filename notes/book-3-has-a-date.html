Book 3 Has a Date

November 20, 2026

Hello, friends.

This has been a big week here at the house, and I have some real news to share.

First, Book 3 has a date. Muffin Goes to the Hospital is now scheduled for release on January 12, 2027. I have been waiting to say that with confidence, and now I can. Muffin has a hospital visit coming, and I think this one will mean a lot to the kids who have had one of their own. I will tell you more as the date gets closer.

The Christmas special is coming along too. I'm Helping! Muffin's Christmas is a story about a little helper right in the middle of it all, and the pictures are getting a last bit of care before it is ready to share. More on that soon.

There is also a first for the house this week: our first card sales are packed and shipped. Thank you to the folks who found them.

And one more small thing. Our first Pinterest pins are live on the Things We Love board, a few of the things we love and use, plus one of our own books. If you are over there, come say hello.

That is the week: a date on the calendar, a Christmas book nearly ready, and our first sales out the door. Thank you for being part of it. Love first, always.

With love,
Jackie
