A Week of Small Shelves Filling Up

November 13, 2026

Love, Mom is out in the world now, paperback and Kindle both, and I am still sitting with that one. It is a letter book, written from my own seeds in my own voice, and sending it out felt like opening the front door and letting someone walk in. Thank you to everyone who has picked it up already. Every copy helps keep this little house standing.

Over on the teen shelf, Chapter 2 of When the Dice Roll went live this week, and Chapter 3, Invoice Day, arrives next Wednesday. Helix is spending her week the way she does, one invoice at a time, and I am having a grand time following her around Tide. If you are reading along, thank you for keeping up with her.

The shop side of the house is finding its feet. The first few listings are up in my little eBay shop, and every sale helps keep the lights on here. And I stepped onto X as @Jwhitemuffin this week, with a pinned post that says hello properly. I want that space to feel like the books do: honest, not pushy, and worth your time.

The Gift Shop shelf is still holding at ten things we love, with a new dog ball launcher joining the row this week. Muffin would have approved. She always did love a good ball.

That is the week. A book out in the world, a chapter on its way, and a shop finding its feet. Love first, always, and thank you for being here with me.

With love,
Jackie
