Twenty New Books, a Chapter, and a Little Mystery

November 18, 2026

Hello, friends.

It is Wednesday, and I have a lot to tell you, so let me start with the chapter. Chapter 3 of When the Dice Roll, called Invoice Day, is out today. Helix is still finding her feet in Tide, and this one is about the ordinary work of keeping a life together, which is where most of the real story lives. If you have been reading along, thank you. Your patience between chapters means the world to me.

Now for the big news. Yesterday I finished a whole new line of log books, twenty of them, and they are all live in the Lulu Bookstore now. There is a Blood Pressure Log Book, a Mileage Log Book for Taxes, a Password Keeper Book, a Dog Health Record Book, a Blood Sugar Log Book, and then a second set: Workout, Fishing, BBQ and Smoking, Vehicle Maintenance, and Hunting. Each one also comes in a men's edition and a women's edition, same pages inside, different covers, so you can pick the one that feels like yours. They are plain, practical books, built to be used every day, and they are all on the Journals, Planners, and More shelf in the Gift Shop.

I also have a new home for all of them. My Lulu Author Spotlight page is live, and it holds every book I have published there, all forty-two of them, newest first, in one place. If you have ever wondered where to find everything at once, that page is the answer.

And one more thing I am excited about. I am starting a comic series for ages seven to twelve, called Mystery Trails, with Muffin and Luna solving gentle mysteries together. The first one is The Case of the Vanishing Treats, and the treats keep disappearing from the porch for a reason I think you will love. I want Muffin to inspire kids the way Wishbone inspired me, with fair clues and readers treated as smart. The outline is done, and the first book is on its way.

The Christmas special is getting closer too. I am Helping! Muffin's Christmas is a story about a little helper right in the middle of it all, and I cannot wait to share it with you when it is ready.

That is the week so far: a chapter out in the world, twenty new books on the shelf, a new home for all of them, and a mystery waiting on the porch. Love first, always, and thank you for being part of it.

With love,
Jackie
