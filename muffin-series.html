Donation commitment

5% of net KDP royalties from the Muffin the Pitbull series are donated every quarter to St. Jude Children's Research Hospital.

This is an ongoing house commitment in Muffin's name, not a one-time campaign. Buying a book from this series supports that quarterly gift. Support is optional beyond the book itself; the stories stay available either way.

Figures are a fixed percent of net KDP royalties, calculated and sent each calendar quarter. No invented dollar totals on this page.
