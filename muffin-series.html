Muffin the Pitbull™ Series

Twenty-six picture books helping kids understand and cope with chronic illness - inspired by Muffin, a real pitbull who had seizures and taught her family what courage looks like.

Donation commitment

5% of net series royalties from the Muffin the Pitbull™ Series are donated every quarter to St. Jude Children's Research Hospital.

This is an ongoing house commitment in Muffin's name, not a one-time campaign. Buying a book from this series supports that quarterly gift. Support is optional beyond the book itself; the stories stay available either way.

For grown-ups: about Muffin's Corner

Muffin's Corner is free play only. It is not a book shop and not a catalogue. Series books and the donation commitment live here on the series page.

Donation language: 5% of net series royalties from the Muffin the Pitbull™ Series are donated every quarter to St. Jude Children's Research Hospital in Muffin's name. Update checkpoints: December 20, March 20, June 20, September 20. No dollar totals are shown in the Corner.

Newsletter (grown-up email signup, not for children to fill in alone): Muffin Newsletter.

Nothing in the Corner asks a child for a name, email, photo, or location. Sound uses recorded read-aloud files and only starts when a child taps.
