Common Questions

Plain answers, no helper required. If your question is not here, a human is. Write to jackiewhite0325@gmail.com.

What is this place?

Sigil and Scribe is a small publishing house run by one person. It is part library, part quiet room: books to read, gentle tools to use, and a children's corner for small hands. There are no accounts to create and nothing here is in a hurry.

What does "Sigil and Scribe" mean?

A sigil is a mark made on purpose: a small symbol meant to carry meaning. A scribe is the person who writes things down and keeps them safe. Put them together and you have this house: we make small marks on purpose, and we write them down. Books, letters, and tools.

Who runs it?

A human does. Jackie White runs the house, with her co-owner Petra Seward. If something on this site helps or confuses you, a person made it and a person answers for it.

Where should I start?

Wherever you are today. The Gift Shop holds the books. The Toolbox holds tools for writers and tired brains. The Scriptorium is the writing room. The Muffin’s Cafe is the grown-ups’ corner and Muffin's Corner is for small children. The house no longer makes custom books.

What is The Scriptorium?

In old monasteries, the scriptorium was the room where scribes copied books by hand. Ours is the writing room on this site, in two weathers: a calm half with a breathing light and quiet writing nooks, and a mischief half with prompt machines and mad libs for wild ideas. It lives in the Muffin’s Cafe.

Where can I buy the books?

Each book's page in the Gift Shop has its Amazon links. Some books are ebooks, some are paperbacks, and each page says which.

Do you make keepsakes or take custom projects?

Not anymore. Custom projects, including keepsakes, are no longer offered in any form. The books in the Library are all yours to enjoy.

Is Muffin's Corner safe for children?

It is built for ages 3 to 7. There are no forms, no sign-ups, and no data collected from children on those pages, ever. Stories can be read aloud with the tap of a button, and everything a child can tap does something gentle.

What is the St. Jude gift on the Muffin books?

Five percent of net royalties from the Muffin the Pitbull series goes to St. Jude Children's Research Hospital, tallied at quarterly checkpoints through the year. The growing total is shown on the Muffin series page.

How do I join the newsletter?

There are two. The Sigil and Scribe Newsletter brings house news about once a month. Muffin's Mailbox is for families who enjoy the children's corner. Both sign-up forms are also linked at the bottom of most pages, and leaving is one click, always.

How do I reach a human?

Email jackiewhite0325@gmail.com. It goes to a human, and a human writes back.

What happened to Pearl?

Pearl was our helper figure, and she has retired. She was cute, but she confused more visitors than she helped, so this page does her job now, in plain words.
