Common Questions

Plain answers, no helper required. If your question is not here, a human is. Start a Project or write to jackiewhite0325@gmail.com.

What is this place?

Sigil and Scribe is a small publishing house run by one person. It is part library, part quiet room: books to read, gentle tools to use, a children's corner, and custom keepsakes made one at a time. There are no accounts to create and nothing here is in a hurry.

What does "Sigil and Scribe" mean?

A sigil is a mark made on purpose: a small symbol meant to carry meaning. A scribe is the person who writes things down and keeps them safe. Put them together and you have this house: we make small marks on purpose, and we write them down. Books, letters, tools, and keepsakes.

Who runs it?

A human does. Jackie White, with her co-owner Petra Seward leading the Zen Den. If something on this site helps or confuses you, a person made it and a person answers for it.

Where should I start?

Wherever you are today. The Library holds the books. The Toolbox holds tools for writers and tired brains. The Zen Den is the quiet corner. The Play Area and Muffin's Corner are for small children and the grown-ups who love them. Keepsakes are custom books made for one person at a time.

What is The Scriptorium?

In old monasteries, the scriptorium was the room where scribes copied books by hand. Ours is the writing room on this site, in two weathers: a calm half with a breathing light and quiet writing nooks, and a mischief half with prompt machines for wild ideas. It lives in the Play Area.

Where can I buy the books?

Each book's page in the Library has its Amazon links. Some books are ebooks, some are paperbacks, and each page says which.

What is a keepsake, and how do I order one?

A keepsake is a custom book made to hold someone you love: a child, a partner, a parent, a friend, a pet, or a comfort object. You fill out a short form, and within a few days you get a written plan and one price. Nothing is owed until the plan reads true to you. After approval, the book is made by hand, revised with you until it feels right, and sent as a PDF, with printing offered after that. Start on the Keepsakes page.

Is Muffin's Corner safe for children?

It is built for ages 3 to 7. There are no forms, no sign-ups, and no data collected from children on those pages, ever. Stories can be read aloud with the tap of a button, and everything a child can tap does something gentle.

What is the St. Jude gift on the Muffin books?

Five percent of net royalties from the Muffin the Pitbull series goes to St. Jude Children's Research Hospital, tallied at quarterly checkpoints through the year. The growing total is shown on the Muffin series page.

How do I join the newsletter?

There are two. The Sigil and Scribe Newsletter brings house news about once a month. Muffin's Mailbox is for families who enjoy the children's corner. Both sign-up forms are also linked at the bottom of most pages, and leaving is one click, always.

How do I reach a human, or start a project?

Use the Start a Project page, or email jackiewhite0325@gmail.com. It goes to a human, and a human writes back.

What happened to Pearl?

Pearl was our helper figure, and she has retired. She was cute, but she confused more visitors than she helped, so this page does her job now, in plain words.
